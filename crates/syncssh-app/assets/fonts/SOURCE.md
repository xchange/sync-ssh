# Bundled terminal fonts

Source: https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/tree/v3.5.1/patched-fonts/JetBrainsMono/Ligatures
Release: Nerd Fonts v3.5.1; upstream JetBrains Mono 2.304.

Unmodified Mono fonts are embedded into the executable and registered before GPUI initializes. They are not installed system-wide. OFL.txt and UPSTREAM.md retain licensing and icon-source attributions.
